# Websites

Language for the four websites and their shared verification work.

## Browser verification

**Snapshot publication**:
Replacement of a site's reviewed visual baselines with snapshots from a successful browser verification run. Publication requires all of that site's required checks to succeed.
_Avoid_: Snapshot upload

## Carolyn portfolio

**Portfolio grid**:
Carolyn's ordered display of project covers or photographs at their original proportions.
_Avoid_: Masonry gallery

## Carolyn photography

**Photography album**:
A named, ordered collection of photographs shown together in Carolyn's photography page.
_Avoid_: Photo category

**Album selection**:
The visitor's choice of a photography album, including a pending choice while its photographs load. If loading fails, the displayed album remains the last successfully displayed choice.
_Avoid_: Album filter state

## Sarabeth playlist

**Playlist preview enrichment**:
The optional addition of small visual previews to Sarabeth's video playlist thumbnails. Unavailable previews do not change which videos are available or their order.
_Avoid_: Playlist enhancement

## Lighthouse checks

**Lighthouse verification**:
A site's local mobile and desktop checks against its required performance, accessibility, best-practices, and SEO minimums. Both profiles must pass for verification to succeed.
_Avoid_: Lighthouse audit job
