# Websites

Language for the four websites and their shared verification work.

## Browser verification

**Snapshot publication**:
Replacement of a site's reviewed visual baselines with snapshots from a successful browser verification run. Publication requires all of that site's required checks to succeed.
_Avoid_: Snapshot upload

## Carolyn photography

**Photography album**:
A named, ordered collection of photographs shown together in Carolyn's photography page.
_Avoid_: Photo category

**Album selection**:
The visitor's choice of a photography album, including a pending choice while its photographs load. If loading fails, the displayed album remains the last successfully displayed choice.
_Avoid_: Album filter state
